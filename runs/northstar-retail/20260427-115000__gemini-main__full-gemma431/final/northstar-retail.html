Modernization Roadmap / Factory

Strategic alignment of application dispositions to a six-lane modernization factory, enabling targeted run-cost reduction and architectural evolution.

Factory Narrative

The modernization roadmap is structured around a factory model that categorizes applications into six distinct disposition lanes. This approach ensures that investment is proportional to the strategic value and technical debt of each asset, aligning delivery ownership with the target operating model.

Wave 1 prioritizes the establishment of observability foundations and the modernization of customer identity, pricing, and returns workflows to resolve immediate pain points in digital conversion and loyalty personalization.

Execution is governed by a strict holiday code freeze from mid-November to early January for all customer-facing platforms, ensuring stability during peak retail traffic. Sequencing is further optimized to manage platform engineering capacity across commerce, loyalty, and supply-chain programs to avoid parallel peak demand.

Delivery Center Architecture

The delivery architecture optimizes for engineering scale and operational proximity, shifting the staffing mix toward offshore hubs to drive cost efficiency while maintaining nearshore alignment for business-critical operations.

Delivery Narrative

The target delivery model transitions from a predominantly onshore footprint (70%) to a balanced global architecture, targeting a 55% offshore mix by Year 3. This shift enables the industrialization of modernization engineering and platform services while preserving high-touch product ownership and architecture governance onshore.

Strategic placement of delivery centers ensures that high-velocity engineering and SRE functions are centralized in India, while business-facing operations and L2 support are anchored in Mexico to maintain critical timezone overlap with North American business units.

This distributed architecture is designed to support the phased modernization waves, ensuring that platform engineering capacity is shared across commerce, loyalty, and supply-chain programs to avoid parallel peak demand during critical release cycles.
